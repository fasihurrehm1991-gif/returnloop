INTRODUCING RETURNLOOP

A small sticker. A big difference.

Losing something important can be stressful. ReturnLoop connects the person who finds your belongings with you through a simple QR code system, making the recovery process easier and more convenient.

SIMPLE PROCESS

How ReturnLoop Works

Getting your belongings back doesn't have to be complicated. Just follow three simple steps.

01

Register

Create your ReturnLoop account and order your personal physical QR sticker.

02

Attach

Place your personal QR sticker on your belongings so they can be identified if lost.

03

Reconnect

Someone who finds your belongings scans the QR code and submits a notification to you.

YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Reconnect without revealing your personal details.

Your privacy is an important part of ReturnLoop. Finders can send a notification without needing access to your personal contact information.

✓ Your phone number is not displayed to finders.
✓ Finders can contact you through the QR notification form.
✓ Receive notifications through your ReturnLoop account.
