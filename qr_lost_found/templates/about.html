ABOUT RETURNLOOP

A simpler way to return lost belongings

ReturnLoop connects the person who finds an item with its owner, without displaying the owner's email address or phone number.

1. Register

Students and staff create an account and provide their institution details.

2. Request a sticker

Choose a package. An administrator confirms the price, payment, and order.

3. Get notified

An administrator issues and activates the QR sticker. A finder can then leave a message for you.

QR images and scan links are kept out of customer dashboards. Only authorized administrators can issue, replace, download, or activate QR codes.
